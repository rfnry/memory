1. Before any step inside a guard, at the mold, the nozzle, the heaters or the hydraulics, the
   technician doing the work locks the machine out, tags it, and verifies zero energy.
2. Barrel, nozzle and heater bands run at up to 300 °C: heat-resistant gloves and a face shield, and
   let a heater cool below 50 °C before touching it.
3. Never loosen a hydraulic fitting under pressure: bleed the accumulator first.
4. An electrical cabinet is opened only by the electrician on call, never by a technician.
5. Every procedure starts with the lockout step and ends with removing the lockout, every time.
