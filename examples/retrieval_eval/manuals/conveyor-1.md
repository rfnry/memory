# Conveyor 1 — quench-to-wash roller conveyor

## Safety

- Test every pull-cord emergency stop at the start of the week.
- Never reach between rollers with the conveyor powered; lock out at panel C-1.

## Maintenance

- Chain tension: check weekly, 10 to 15 mm of sag at the midpoint.
- Roller bearings: grease monthly.
