# Press 2 — die change procedure, rev B (issued 2026-05-12, supersedes rev A)

1. Bring the ram to the bottom of its stroke and set the ram safety block.
2. Lock out at D-14 and open the dump valve.
3. Remove the die clamp bolts (M24, eight of them) and roll the die out on the die cart. The cart
   takes at most 3 tonnes.
4. Fit the new die, then tighten the clamp bolts to 410 N·m in a cross pattern.
5. Re-torque the clamp bolts after the first 50 strokes.
6. Sign the die log before the press returns to production.
