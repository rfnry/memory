# Furnace 1 — gas-fired heat-treat furnace

## Safety

- Never open the door above 400 C except through the charge cycle.
- Face shield and heat gloves at the quench tank, always.
- A gas smell: shut the manual gas valve G-3 at the wall, leave the bay, call the lead.

## Maintenance

- Burners: inspect weekly for flame colour and nozzle deposits.
- Thermocouples: calibrate every quarter against the reference probe; replace any reading more
  than 3 C off.
- Door seals: replace every 12 months.
