# Press 1 — 250-ton mechanical stamping press

## Safety

- Lock out at main disconnect D-12 and let the flywheel stop fully before reaching into the die
  area. The flywheel coasts for about 90 seconds.
- Test the light curtain at the start of every shift with the test rod. If it fails, the press
  stays locked out until maintenance signs it off. Never run on two-hand controls alone.

## Maintenance

- Crown gears: grease every 160 running hours.
- Clutch and brake air: 5.5 bar, checked daily at start of shift.
- Gearbox oil (ISO VG 220): change every 2,000 running hours.
