# Press 2 — 400-ton hydraulic press

## Safety

- Lock out at main disconnect D-14 and open the hydraulic dump valve until the gauge reads zero
  before any work under the ram.
- Put the ram safety block in place whenever the ram is not at the top of its stroke.

## Maintenance

- Hydraulic oil ISO VG 46; change the return filter every 500 running hours.
- Accumulator precharge: 90 bar nitrogen, checked monthly.
- Oil temperature above 60 C trips the press; let it cool, do not reset it hot.
