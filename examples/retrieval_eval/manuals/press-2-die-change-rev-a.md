# Press 2 — die change procedure, rev A (issued 2024-03-04)

1. Bring the ram to the bottom of its stroke and set the ram safety block.
2. Lock out at D-14 and open the dump valve.
3. Remove the die clamp bolts (M24, eight of them) and roll the die out on the die cart.
4. Fit the new die, then tighten the clamp bolts to 380 N·m in a cross pattern.
5. Re-torque the clamp bolts after the first 100 strokes.
