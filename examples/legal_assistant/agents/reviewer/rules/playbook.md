---
description: The positions the firm takes for a client buying services, clause by clause
---
The firm's client is the customer buying the services.

1. Limitation of liability: the supplier's cap is at least the fees paid in the 12 months before the
   claim, and a breach of confidentiality or data protection is outside the cap.
2. Indemnity: mutual, each party covering third-party claims caused by its own breach. An indemnity
   given by the client alone is a high risk.
3. Termination: the client may terminate for convenience on 30 days' notice or less, and either
   party for a breach not cured within 15 days.
4. Confidentiality: mutual, and lasting at least 3 years after the agreement ends.
5. Payment: invoices due in 30 days or more; late interest of 1% a month at most.
6. Governing law: the law of the client's home state.
