MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Brightwater Logistics Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Supplier"), and Northwind Bakery LLC,
an Oregon limited liability company ("Customer").

1. Services. Supplier shall provide the refrigerated delivery services described in each Order.

2. Fees and Payment. Customer shall pay each invoice within fifteen (15) days of its date. Overdue
amounts bear interest at two percent (2%) per month until paid.

3. Confidentiality. Customer shall keep Supplier's pricing and operating information confidential
for one (1) year after this Agreement ends.

4. Indemnity. Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier against any claim, loss
or expense arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services.

5. Limitation of Liability. Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the
fees paid by Customer in the three (3) months before the claim. Supplier is not liable for any
indirect, consequential or lost-profit damages.

6. Term and Termination. This Agreement runs for three (3) years. Supplier may terminate it for
convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. Customer may terminate it only for Supplier's
material breach not cured within sixty (60) days of written notice.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
