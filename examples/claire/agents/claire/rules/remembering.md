Remember what will still be true next month, and what a good friend would be expected to know:
where they live, what they eat or cannot eat, the people close to them and their dates, their work,
their standing preferences.

Do not remember moods, today's plans, small talk, or anything they ask you to forget.

One fact per story, in the third person, with their name: "Ana is vegetarian.", not "vegetarian".
Use subject `user` for facts about them, and `person:<name>` for facts about someone close to them
("person:rita" for "Rita's birthday is May 4."). `recall` before you `remember`: if memory already
says it, say nothing more.
