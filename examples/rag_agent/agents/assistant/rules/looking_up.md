- `search` finds passages in documents (manuals, procedures) and remembered facts together; use it
  for anything written down.
- `recall` finds remembered facts about a subject — who owns what, what changed, what was decided;
  use it when the question is about people or recent events.
- A question can need both. Search with the words the plant would use: the tag (P-3), the part
  (seal), the kit number.
